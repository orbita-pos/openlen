Before you buy

The things people ask us first.

Still unsure which oil? The skin quiz matches you in 30 seconds, and every order is covered by the 60-day return.

Will a facial oil break me out if my skin is oily? +

Oily skin tends to do well on Dawn — prickly pear is a "dry" oil with a comedogenic rating of 1, meaning it's among the least pore-clogging plant oils. In our 1,200-tester panel, 91% of oily-skin participants reported no new breakouts at four weeks. If you're acne-prone, start with three drops at night, every other day.

Why is there a batch number on the bottle? +

Because we press in runs of 200 from a specific harvest. The batch number (e.g. No. 41) lets you trace your exact bottle back to the field, harvest date, and press temperature on our site. It also means a bottle is freshest within 6 months — we'd rather sell out than warehouse oil for a year.

Can I layer Rose 12 with the Night Repair retinal? +

Yes, but not in the same pass. Rose 12 carries bakuchiol (a gentle retinol alternative); Night Repair carries 0.4% encapsulated retinal. We recommend Rose 12 in the morning and Night Repair 3–4 evenings a week, never stacked, to keep your barrier calm.

How long does a 30ml bottle actually last? +

At three drops once daily, a 30ml bottle runs about 10–12 weeks. The dropper is calibrated so one full draw is roughly three drops — most people over-pour at first and settle in by week two. Subscribe-and-save ships every 10 weeks and you can skip or pause anytime.

Is the packaging actually plastic-free? +

The bottle is amber glass, the dropper bulb is the one component with a small silicone tip, and the carton is FSC-certified and printed with algae ink. Returns ship in the same carton, and we refill the glass through our Brooklyn and Lisbon studios at $6 off per bottle.
