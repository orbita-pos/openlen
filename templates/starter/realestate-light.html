Questions

What buyers and sellers ask at the first call.

How is your commission structured on a sale? +

Listings are a flat 2.5% to Harborline plus a negotiated buyer-agent co-op — typically 2–2.5%. We put the full split in writing before you sign, and we rebate 0.5% back to sellers who also buy their next home through the desk within 12 months.

Do you show off-market and pocket listings? +

Yes. Roughly a third of our annual volume never touches the MLS. Once we understand your budget and timeline, we share the private inventory — including homes where the owner is testing the market quietly before committing to a public listing.

How do you price a coastal home with so few comps? +

We blend the last 24 months of closed sales within a half-mile, adjust per-square-foot for view corridor and lot frontage, and pressure-test against active competition. On unique parcels we commission an independent appraisal before listing so the number survives the buyer's lender.

Can you handle a 1031 exchange or a trust sale? +

Routinely. We coordinate directly with your qualified intermediary, estate attorney, and CPA to hold the 45- and 180-day clocks. About 40% of our seller side last year involved a trust, conservatorship, or exchange — we keep a vetted bench for each.

What does the marketing on a listing actually include? +

Architectural photography, twilight and drone sets, a 3D Matterport walk-through, a single-property microsite, and a print piece mailed to 1,400 households within the view band. We don't charge it back at close — it's part of the 2.5%.
