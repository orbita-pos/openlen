Questions

Before you book the call.

The things founders ask us in the first email — answered up front.

Do you actually build the site, or just design it? +

We build. Marketing sites ship in Next.js + Tailwind to your repo, and product UI ships as production components against a real design system. You get a running site, not a Figma file to hand to a contractor.

What happens if scope changes mid-sprint? +

Small reprioritisations inside the agreed scope are free — we'd rather build the right thing. A genuine scope expansion becomes a fixed-price change order you sign before we start it. No silent overage on the invoice.

Who owns the work and the source files? +

You do — fully, on final payment. That includes the Figma file, the brand book, fonts licensed in your name, and a clean Git history. We keep nothing locked behind us.

Can you work with our in-house engineers? +

Often the best outcome. We pair on the component architecture, open PRs against your conventions, and leave behind a system your team can extend. About a third of our engagements are co-build with an in-house team.

How far out are you booking? +

We run two Studio engagements at a time, so the calendar fills four to six weeks ahead. Sprints can sometimes slot in sooner. The intro call is where we confirm a real start date — never a vague "soon."
