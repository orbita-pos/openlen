Got something we missed? Email a human.

Why 75mg caffeine and not more?
We calibrated it for sustained focus, not a one-hour spike. 75mg is roughly one strong cup of coffee — enough to feel sharp, not enough to make your hands shake. Most "energy" drinks pile on 200+mg because they're masking poor formulation with a stimulant cliff.
Is it actually zero sugar?
Yes — sweetened with monk fruit, stevia, and a touch of erythritol. Tastes like real sugar, doesn't act like it. Glycemic index of zero, no insulin response, no afternoon crash from a blood-sugar dive.
Where's it made?
Boulder, Colorado. Small-batch production at our own facility — every run is third-party-tested before it ships. You can read every batch report at lume.co/reports, with dates and lot numbers.
Can I drink Lume every day?
Yes. One or two cans daily is plenty for most adults — well under the FDA's 400mg-caffeine daily threshold. Stay hydrated, eat real food, sleep well. We're a tool, not a meal replacement.
What's your sustainability angle?
100% recyclable aluminum cans (aluminum recycles infinitely — better than PET). Plant-based labels printed with vegetable inks. 1% of revenue routed to environmental nonprofits annually — last year that was $186,400, mostly to American Rivers and 1% for the Planet.
