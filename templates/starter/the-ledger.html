Questions readers ask

Is this financial advice?+

No. The Ledger is journalism and analysis, not personalized investment advice. Nothing here is a recommendation to buy or sell anything — it's framing to help you ask better questions before you talk to someone licensed.

Free vs paid — what do I miss on free?+

Free readers get the Open and one chart every Sunday. Subscribers get the full letter (usually 3–4× longer), the midweek macro note, the complete archive, and every chart as downloadable CSV.

Cadence — really just Sundays?+

The main letter lands every Sunday at 8am ET without fail. Subscribers also get a short midweek note when something moves enough to warrant it — never more than twice a week, total.

Can my employer expense it?+

Yes — an itemized receipt arrives the moment you subscribe, and there's a team rate for finance and operations groups who want it on one invoice.

Will you ever sell or rent my email?+

Never. No lists sold, no data brokers, no "partner offers." The only thing that ever arrives from this address is the letter, and you can leave with one click.
