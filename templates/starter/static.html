// ABOUT

WHO WE
ARE

Reverb Front formed in a practice space above a print shop in early 2024 — four people who wanted songs short enough to finish before anyone could leave. STATIC is the band's third EP in eighteen months, after DEAD AIR (2024) and SIGNAL LOSS (2025). Five songs, seven minutes, tracked live to tape over a single afternoon with no overdubs and no click.

There is no full-length on the calendar and there isn't going to be one. The band believes a record should say what it needs to say and then stop — so STATIC stops at 7:22, pressed to a 500-copy 7" with no plans for a repress. If you want it on wax, get it now; if you want to hear it loud, the room is the only place that counts.
