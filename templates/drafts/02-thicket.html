Good questions

Things homeowners ask us

Do you do one-time cleanups or only ongoing plans?

Both. We’ll happily handle a single spring or fall cleanup with no strings attached. Many homeowners try us with a one-time job and roll into a weekly plan after — but that’s your call, never a requirement.

Who designs the planting — a real designer?

Yes — Mara Ellison, our staff landscape designer, draws every planting plan herself. She visits your property, checks the light and soil, and hand-draws a layout. You get a real plan, not a stock template a salesperson printed.

Are you licensed and insured for tree work?

Fully. Thicket carries general liability and workers’ comp, and tree work is done or supervised by our ISA-certified arborist. We’ll send proof of insurance before any job — never let an uninsured crew near trees by your house.

Do you offer organic lawn treatments?

We do. Alongside our standard program we offer a fully organic, compost-and-microbe-based lawn treatment — safer for kids, pets and pollinators. It costs a little more and takes a season to hit its stride, and we’ll be upfront about both.

What’s the lead time for a patio build?

In peak season, plan on three to six weeks from a signed estimate to the first day on site, then four to eight working days to build, weather permitting. Book in winter and you’ll usually get an earlier spring slot.
