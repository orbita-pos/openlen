Before you buy

The honest answers.

Five questions we get most. No marketing dodge.

Is there a subscription, and what do I lose without it?

No subscription is required, and nothing core is locked behind one. Your $229 buys the band and the full core app — the daily readiness score, the complete sleep-stage breakdown, HRV, resting heart rate, skin temperature and blood-oxygen — and that works forever. Loop+ is a purely optional $6/month add-on that layers on the long-term trend archive and a written daily coaching summary. If you never subscribe, you lose exactly those two things and nothing else.

How accurate is the sleep-stage tracking?

Loop's four-stage staging (deep, REM, light, awake) was validated against clinical polysomnography in a 318-night study and reached 87% epoch-by-epoch agreement — strong for a wrist wearable, though no consumer device matches a sleep lab exactly. It uses the PPG optical array, the accelerometer and skin temperature together, which is far more reliable than motion alone. For diagnosing a sleep disorder, a clinical study is still the right tool, and the app can export a clean report to bring to one.

How is my health data stored — is it ever sold?

Your data is yours. It is encrypted in transit and at rest, and it is never sold, never rented, and never shared with advertisers or data brokers — that is a written commitment, not a setting you have to find. You can export everything as a PDF or CSV at any time, and deleting your account permanently erases your records from our servers within 30 days. We make money from selling the band, not from your sleep.

Can I wear it in the shower or swimming?

Yes to both. Loop is water-resistant to 100 metres (10 ATM), so showers, pools and open-water swims are all fine — there is nothing to take off. The titanium-and-TPU shell has no charging ports exposed to water; it charges by magnetic contact on the dock. We'd only steer you clear of scuba diving at depth and high-pressure jets, which fall outside the 10 ATM rating.

Does it have a screen or notifications?

No — and that is the point. Loop has no display and pushes no texts, calls or app alerts to your wrist. The band only senses; the app on your phone is the screen. The single nudge it can send is an optional, gentle wind-down reminder tuned to your readiness, and you can switch even that off. It is built so you check in once a day, not all day.
