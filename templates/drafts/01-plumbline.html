FAQ

Straight answers, before you call

The questions Brooklyn homeowners ask us most. Still unsure? We're happy to talk it through on the phone.

Call (555) 408-2210
Do you charge for the estimate?

No. Estimates are free for standard residential work — we come out, look at the job, and give you a written flat-rate quote with zero obligation. The only exception is a sewer-line camera inspection, which is a diagnostic service priced from $189, and that fee is credited toward the repair if you book it with us.

Are you available nights and weekends?

Yes. Our regular hours are Mon-Sat, 7am to 8pm, and we keep a 24/7 emergency line open every night, Sunday and holiday for burst pipes, major leaks and no-water situations. A real plumber answers — not a machine — and best of all, there's no overtime surcharge for after-hours calls.

Is pricing by the hour or flat-rate?

Always flat-rate. We diagnose the problem first, then quote one fixed price for the whole job, parts and labor included. You approve that number before any work starts, and it doesn't move — if the job takes us longer than expected, that's on us, not you. No meter, no padded hours.

Do you pull the permit for water-heater work?

Yes. As a licensed master plumber, Plumbline pulls the required NYC permit for every water-heater install and schedules the inspection on your behalf. The permit cost is itemized in your flat-rate quote up front — no hidden fees. Skipping the permit can void your home insurance, so we never do.

What's the warranty difference between a repair and an install?

Both carry our 2-year workmanship warranty — if our labor fails within two years, we return and correct it at no charge. The difference is the parts: a new install (water heater, fixture, sump pump) also carries the manufacturer's product warranty, often 6 to 12 years, which we register for you. On a repair to existing equipment, we warranty our work, but we can't warranty aging parts we didn't supply.
