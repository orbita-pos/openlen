Questions

Before you buy

Do I need a license to fly it commercially?

Yes — commercial drone operation requires a remote-pilot certificate in most countries (a Part 107 certificate in the US, an A2 or GVC in the EU). Skylark itself sits in the sub-25 kg class, so it does not need special airframe certification. You are responsible for registering the aircraft and respecting local airspace rules.

How accurate are the maps — is it truly survey-grade?

With an RTK fix Skylark holds 1 cm horizontal and vertical absolute accuracy, plus 1 ppm for distance from the base. Across 312 verification flights it matched survey-grade checkpoints with no ground control points placed. For legally binding cadastral work we still recommend a handful of independent checkpoints — best practice, not a limitation of the drone.

Is cloud processing required, or can it run offline?

Cloud is optional. The Skylark app processes orthomosaics, point clouds, and volumes entirely on-device, which matters on remote sites with no signal. Skylark Cloud is faster for very large datasets and adds team sharing and version history — the Survey Kit includes a year of it, and it is a paid add-on otherwise.

What's the obstacle-avoidance coverage?

Six-direction binocular vision covers forward, backward, left, right, up, and down, detecting obstacles out to roughly 30 m. During automated missions Skylark will slow, route around, or hold position rather than push through. Vision systems struggle with thin wires and in low light, so keep visual line of sight and plan conservative altitudes near infrastructure.

How are repairs and crash replacements handled?

The 2-year warranty covers manufacturing defects. Accidental damage is handled through Skylark Care: a flat repair fee, or a replacement airframe shipped within five business days while we assess yours. Send the flight log with your claim — it speeds diagnosis, and props and landing gear are inexpensive consumables you can swap yourself.
