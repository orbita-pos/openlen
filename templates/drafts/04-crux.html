Before you climb

First-timer questions.

I've never climbed — do I need a class first?+

No. Bouldering is climbing close to the ground over thick pads — no ropes, no partner, no certification. Walk in and staff will cover falling and the basics. The free Intro to Climbing session is there if you want more structure.

What gear do I need to rent or buy?+

Just climbing shoes and chalk — both rent for $8 together, or free on your first-visit deal. Most climbers buy their own shoes within a month or two; we sell them, and members get 20% off.

Is there a fitness area beyond climbing?+

Yes — a full training zone with hangboards, a campus board, rings, and free weights, plus three yoga classes a week. All of it is included with a monthly membership.

How often do routes change?+

Constantly. Our five setters put up fresh problems every Friday, rotating two zones a week — so the whole gym, all 240 problems, turns over about once a month.

Is there a kids' program?+

Yes — a coached Youth Team for ages 7 to 17, from recreational squads to a competitive team, plus open climbing for families. Kids under 14 climb with a supervising adult.
