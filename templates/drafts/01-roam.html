The mower

Engineered to be forgotten about.

Three things make Roam different from the robot mowers that came before it — no buried wire, a deck that actually cuts, and a lock that travels with the machine.

NO BOUNDARY WIRE

Wire-free RTK-GPS navigation

No trench, no buried perimeter wire, no weekend with a spade. Walk the edge of your lawn once in the app and Roam holds that boundary to within two centimetres — every mow, in every season, as the grass grows in.

Twin RTK antennas correct each other in real time, so it stays accurate under trees and beside the house where plain GPS drifts.

Boundary accuracy ±2 cm · setup ~10 min

A real mower, not a trimmer

Three pivoting razor blades on a 22 cm deck mulch clippings back into the lawn as fertiliser — no bag to empty. All-wheel drive pulls Roam up slopes as steep as 24°, and a rain sensor sends it back to the dock when a downpour starts, so it never tracks mud across wet grass.

Theft tracking and a PIN lock

A built-in LTE radio means Roam is reachable even with the Wi-Fi off. Set a geofence around your property and you get a push alert the moment the mower crosses it — with a live location you can hand straight to police.

Lift it off the lawn and a four-digit PIN is required before the blades will spin again. To a thief it’s a tracked, locked brick — which is exactly the point.

Before you buy

The questions owners ask first.

Still unsure? The support team answers email in under a day — details in the footer below.

Do I need to bury a boundary wire?

No. Roam navigates with RTK-GPS, accurate to about two centimetres, so there is no perimeter wire to trench, staple or repair. You set the lawn up once: open the app, walk Roam slowly around the edges, and mark any flower beds or features to avoid. That virtual boundary is saved and reused for every mow — and you can redraw it in seconds if your garden changes.

How does it handle slopes and obstacles?

All-wheel drive lets Roam climb inclines up to 24 degrees — steeper than most suburban back gardens. A front bumper sensor detects fixed objects like trees, planters and garden furniture, so it slows, contacts gently, then steers around them. Lift and tilt sensors stop the blades instantly if it is picked up or tips, and the rain sensor parks it on the dock during heavy rain.

Is it safe around kids and pets?

Safety drove the design. The three blades sit recessed well inside the chassis and pivot back on contact rather than driving through. A lift sensor cuts blade power within milliseconds the moment Roam leaves the ground, and the bumper makes it ease off when it meets anything solid. It also moves at a deliberate walking pace. We still recommend keeping very young children supervised in the garden — as you would around any powered tool.

Is there an app subscription?

None. The Roam app is free on iOS and Android, and every feature — zone mapping, scheduling, Mow now, mow history and over-the-air firmware updates — is included for the life of the mower. The built-in LTE that powers theft tracking is covered too: there is no separate SIM to buy and no monthly connectivity fee. The price you see is the only price.

What’s the warranty, and how are repairs handled?

Every Roam comes with a 3-year warranty covering the mower, the battery and the charging dock for both parts and labour. If something fails, start a claim in the app or by email and we send a prepaid shipping box — most repairs are turned around within five working days. Wear items such as blades are not covered, but spares are inexpensive and the box includes nine to start. You also have 30 days from delivery to return Roam for a full refund.
