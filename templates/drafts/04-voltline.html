FAQ

Questions homeowners ask us.

Still wondering about something? Call (555) 512-3344 and an electrician will pick up.

Do you pull the permit and handle the inspection?

Yes. For permitted work we pull the electrical permit with the City of Austin, schedule the inspection, and meet the inspector on-site ourselves. You don't have to coordinate anything — and our first-time pass rate is 100%.

What level of EV charger can you install?

We install Level 2 (240V) chargers — the standard for home charging — including hardwired units and NEMA 14-50 outlets. We check your panel capacity first and size the circuit to your vehicle, so it charges at full speed without overloading anything.

Is the diagnostic fee credited toward the work?

Yes. The $89 diagnostic visit covers an electrician's time to find the actual problem and write up a fix. If you hire us to do that repair, the full $89 comes straight off the invoice — so the diagnosis effectively costs you nothing.

Can you bring an old house up to code?

Absolutely — it's a lot of what we do in central Austin's older neighborhoods. Knob-and-tube, ungrounded outlets, undersized panels, missing GFCI protection: we assess what's there, prioritize the safety items, and bring it to current code in stages that fit your budget.

Do you do panel upgrades for a solar install?

We do. Many solar installers need the main panel upgraded or a dedicated backfeed breaker added before they can interconnect. We coordinate with your solar company, do the panel work to code, and make sure it's inspection-ready for the PV tie-in.
