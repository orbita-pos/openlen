The system

Three parts. One install. No jargon.

A Sunhouse is panels, a battery, and an app that ties them together — sized to your roof and your bill, then installed by our own crews in a single visit.

01 · Panels

Panels that earn their roof space

High-efficiency monocrystalline panels pull more power from every square metre — so a smaller, tidier array covers the same bill. We model your roof's pitch, shading, and orientation before a single panel is ordered.

440 W per panel 22.3% cell efficiency 25 yr output guarantee
02 · Battery

Power that stays when the grid doesn't

When the utility drops, the Sunhouse battery takes over in under 20 milliseconds — fast enough that your lights, fridge, and Wi-Fi never blink. Stored sun runs the house through the outage and recharges the next morning.

< 20 ms transfer 13.5 kWh usable 90% round-trip

Start at 13.5 kWh, grow to 40.5

Begin with one battery and stack up to three as your needs change — an EV in the driveway, a heat pump, a longer outage you'd rather sleep through. The app shifts your usage into the cheapest hours automatically, so the system pays its own way.

Honest answers

Before you book the visit

The questions homeowners actually ask us on the first call.

How much will this actually cut my bill? +

It depends on your roof, your usage, and your utility's rates — which is exactly what the free design visit measures. Across our installs, homeowners see an average first-year reduction of $1,884, and many sunny-state homes cut 70–90% of their grid bill. Your design report shows a specific estimate for your address before you commit to anything.

Does it keep the lights on in a blackout — for how long? +

Yes. When the grid drops, the battery takes over in under 20 milliseconds — fast enough that nothing in the house resets. A single 13.5 kWh battery covers an average home for around 14 hours of essentials, and through daylight the panels recharge it. Stack a second or third battery if you want to run more, or ride out longer outages without rationing.

Who handles the permits and the utility paperwork? +

We do — it's part of the fixed quote, not an add-on. Sunhouse prepares and files the city building and electrical permits, and submits the utility interconnection application for your net-metering agreement and meter swap. We track each one through to approval and keep you posted in the app. In most cases you sign one or two forms and nothing else.

Can I add more battery later? +

Yes — the system is built to grow. You can start with one 13.5 kWh battery and stack up to three units for a total of 40.5 kWh. The Helios H7 inverter and Sunlink gateway already support the full stack, so adding a battery later is a short visit, not a re-engineering job. Many homeowners expand when they buy an EV or add a heat pump.

What's covered by the 12-year warranty? +

The 12-year system warranty covers the battery, the Helios H7 inverter, the Sunlink gateway, and the Sunhouse installation workmanship — including the roof penetrations we make. Panels carry a separate 25-year output guarantee, holding at least 87% of rated power at year 25. Our 24/7 monitoring usually flags a fault before you notice it, and warranty service is scheduled at no charge.
