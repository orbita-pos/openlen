OUR STORY

We were tired of the water-or-beer choice.

Highball started with two friends — Maya and Theo — at a backyard party in 2021, both nursing the same lukewarm sparkling water because nothing on the table felt like an actual drink. The sodas were too sweet. The mocktails were a sad afterthought.

So they spent a year in a rented kitchen working with a botanist and a flavor house, pressing real fruit, steeping bittering botanicals, and tasting hundreds of versions until a can felt grown-up — bittersweet, dry, complex — without leaning on sugar to get there.

The one thing they refused to compromise on: it had to taste like something you'd choose, not something you'd settle for. Five grams of cane sugar, real botanicals you can name, and nothing you'd need to look up. That's the whole brief.

— Maya & Theo
Co-founders

WHAT'S INSIDE

Read the can. Then read ours.

No mystery ingredients, no chemistry-set sweeteners. If it's in the can, it's on this list.

What's in it

  • Real cold-pressed botanicals — steeped, not flavored: gentian root, hibiscus, fresh basil, bay.
  • A touch of real fruit — pressed grapefruit, yuzu, plum and pomelo for body, not just color.
  • 5g of cane sugar — just enough to round the bitterness, nothing more.
  • 35 calories a can — light enough for the fourth one.

What's not

  • ✕Alcohol — zero proof, full stop. Safe for the designated driver and the pregnant friend.

  • ✕Sucralose or acesulfame-K — no artificial sweeteners, no weird aftertaste.
  • ✕Vague "natural flavors" you can't trace — every ingredient is named on the can.
  • ✕39g of sugar — the amount in a regular cola. We use 5.

FAQ

The honest answers.

Is it actually non-alcoholic? +

Completely. Highball contains 0.0% alcohol by volume — it's never fermented and there's no trace amount, unlike some "alcohol-removed" drinks. It's safe and intended for the designated driver, anyone pregnant, sober folks, and anyone who simply doesn't feel like drinking tonight.

How much sugar is in it? +

Five grams of cane sugar per 12oz can, which works out to 35 calories. For comparison, a regular cola has around 39 grams. We use just enough sugar to round off the bitterness of the botanicals — and no artificial sweeteners like sucralose or acesulfame-K to fake the rest.

What are the botanicals — is there caffeine? +

Real cold-pressed botanicals — things like gentian root, hibiscus, fresh basil and bay — steeped for flavor and a pleasant bitterness, plus a touch of pressed fruit. There is zero caffeine in any flavor. Every botanical and fruit is named on the can, so nothing is hiding behind "natural flavors."

Is it sold by the case? +

Yes — three ways. Singles at $2.80, an 8-pack at $21.00, and a 24-pack case at $57.60, which is our best value per can. You can mix any flavors within a pack, so a case can be all Grapefruit & Yuzu or one of everything. Orders over $35 ship free.

Is the can recyclable? +

Fully. Our 12oz cans are 100% aluminum, infinitely recyclable, and made with a high share of recycled content. Just rinse and pop it in your curbside bin — no liner to peel, no mixed materials. Shipping cartons are recyclable corrugated cardboard too.
