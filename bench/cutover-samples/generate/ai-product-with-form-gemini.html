FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How does Calliope learn our company's specific knowledge?

Calliope connects to your existing knowledge bases like Notion, Confluence, or Zendesk Guide. It continuously syncs to learn your product, policies, and best practices. You can also provide it with conversation histories to learn your team's unique voice and tone.

Is our customer data secure?

Yes. We are SOC 2 Type II compliant and all data is encrypted at rest and in transit. We use sub-processors who meet our stringent security standards. Your data is used exclusively to power your instance of Calliope and is never shared or used for training models for other customers.

What platforms does Calliope integrate with?

We have native integrations for Zendesk, Intercom, Front, and Salesforce Service Cloud. We also offer a robust API for custom integrations with any other help desk or communication platform.

How does automated resolution work?

You define rules and confidence thresholds. When Calliope is highly confident it has the correct answer to a customer's question (e.g., "how do I reset my password?"), it can respond and close the ticket automatically. For more complex or sensitive issues, it will draft a reply and assign it to a human agent for review.

Can we customize the AI's personality?

Absolutely. You can provide Calliope with brand guidelines and examples of ideal conversations. It will adapt its tone, phrasing, and even emoji usage to perfectly match your brand's voice, ensuring a consistent customer experience.
