FAQ

Common questions

Can I import issues from Jira or Linear?
Yes. We offer one-click importers for Jira, Linear, Asana, and GitHub Issues. You can import all your historical data, including comments, attachments, and labels, in just a few minutes.
How is a "user" defined for billing?
A user is any member of your workspace with create, edit, or comment permissions. We do not charge for read-only guest accounts. You are only billed for active users during a given billing cycle.
Do you offer a self-hosted option?
Self-hosting is available for customers on our Scale plan. We provide a Docker container that can be deployed into your own VPC. Please contact sales for more information on system requirements and pricing.
How does the AI triage feature work?
Our AI model is trained on a vast dataset of open-source projects. When a new issue is created, the model analyzes its title and description to predict the appropriate labels (e.g., bug, feature, chore), priority, and potential assignee based on your team's past activity and code ownership.
