Café de origen, con alma de barrio.

In the heart of Roma Norte, Caracol is a refuge for coffee lovers. We source single-origin beans directly from fincas in Chiapas and Oaxaca, roasting in-house to bring out the unique story in every cup.

NUESTRAS RAÍCES

Direct from the Finca

We believe great coffee begins with a great relationship. We partner directly with small-lot farmers in Mexico’s most celebrated coffee regions, ensuring fair prices and sustainable practices. Each bean is a testament to the land and the hands that harvested it.

  • Pluma Hidalgo, Oaxaca — Notes of chocolate, almond, and citrus from high-altitude Typica varietals.
  • Jaltenango, Chiapas — A balanced cup with hints of stone fruit and brown sugar from Bourbon and Caturra beans.
  • Coatepec, Veracruz — Bright, floral, and complex; a showcase of the region's rich volcanic soil.
