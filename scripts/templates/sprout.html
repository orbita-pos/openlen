Accent training

Hear your own accent — and fix it.

Each spoken sentence is graded on pronunciation by syllable. You see where your accent diverges from native, hear the difference back-to-back, and re-record until it lands. Not perfectionism — just specific.

  • →Per-syllable breakdown. "u-ni-ver-si-dad" → which syllable was off, by how much.
  • →A/B native playback. Tap to hear your version, then native, side-by-side, instantly.
  • →Region-specific scoring. If you chose Buenos Aires Spanish, you're scored against that, not Madrid.

FAQ

What every adult learner asks first.

How is this different from Duolingo, Babbel, Pimsleur?

Duolingo gamifies vocabulary; you can't speak after a year. Pimsleur is audio-only and excellent for accent but slow on grammar. Babbel is solid for written exercises but rarely produces conversation. Sprout's core difference is the weekly AI conversations and per-syllable accent feedback — those two things make speaking the goal, not a side-effect. About 28% of our power users came from Duolingo specifically, and 84% of consistent users hit B1/B2 by month four; that's not a number Duolingo or Babbel publish.

Is the AI tutor a real voice or synthetic?

Synthetic, generated from a recording of a real native speaker who consented + is credited and paid royalties per conversation. The voice you hear as "Andrés" is built on hundreds of hours of recordings by a real Madrileño actor named Andrés Fuentes. We use modern voice models that handle prosody and regional intonation — most users tell us they couldn't tell within the first conversation, and the ones who could didn't mind.

Can I practice for DELE / JLPT / DELF?

Yes — Plus members can switch into an exam track for DELE (Spanish), JLPT N5–N1 (Japanese), DELF/DALF (French), HSK (Mandarin), and Goethe (German). The track replaces the default daily lessons with exam-aligned reading + listening + writing exercises, plus a final mock test with timing. About 14,000 of our members have passed an exam they prepped for on Sprout, JLPT N3 being the most common.

Does it work offline on long flights?

Plus members get a 7-day offline cache — lessons, listening exercises, vocab review all work without network. AI conversations require a connection (we run them server-side for latency), so you can't speak to Andrés mid-flight, but you can do everything else. Progress syncs the next time you're online.

What languages are coming next?

Hindi is launching in July 2026 (already in beta with 2,400 members). Vietnamese and Greek are slated for Q4. Hebrew is being scoped. We don't add languages until we have the native tutor talent + scenario library at the level the existing 12 ship at — half-baked language support is worse than no support, and we've held that line since launch.
