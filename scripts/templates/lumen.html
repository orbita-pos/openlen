FAQ

The questions sleep-deprived adults actually ask.

Does it work without headphones?

Yes. Every track is mixed for phone speakers too — we test on a 2018 iPhone, not just AirPods Pro. Some users actually prefer it: the soft speaker hiss adds a layer of ambient. For partners, there's a 'low-bedside' mix that drops the bass and EQs above 80 Hz so it carries less.

Can I use it offline on flights?

Plus members can download any wind-down, story, or ambient track for 30 days of offline playback. The Tonight program needs network for the initial build, but once it's built, the whole sequence is cached locally. Long-haul tested by our team flying SFO–SYD.

How does this compare to Calm or Headspace?

Calm and Headspace are libraries — you pick the thing. Lumen builds you a 30–90 minute sequence each night based on inputs about your day and (optionally) your wearable. We're smaller, focused only on sleep, and we don't ship daytime meditation, courses, or kids' content. If you want a broad mindfulness app, Calm is great. If you want one job done well at 11pm, that's us.

What's the cancellation flow?

Settings → Subscription → Cancel. Two taps. No retention screen, no "wait, here's 50% off!", no email gauntlet. App Store / Play Store cancellations work the same — and we don't charge for the remainder of your billing period, you just keep Plus until then.

Are the voices AI-generated or human?

100% human. Every narrator records in a treated booth in London, Brooklyn, or Tokyo. We considered AI for the long-tail languages but decided against it — the micro-pauses and breath patterns that actually help you drift off are exactly what synthetic voices miss. Each narrator is paid per session and credited.
