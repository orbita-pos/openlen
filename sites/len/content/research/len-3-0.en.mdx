<h2 id="agent">Understand, plan and check</h2>

Len's capabilities are also in **how it carries a task forward**. It can consult project files and conversations, clarify what is missing, organize a longer task and choose the tools it needs. Planning, context and checking come together with execution and the new deliverables in the same workspace.

**Understand the goal.** When it needs your decision, it can ask with options. It clarifies the task with you rather than guessing that missing detail. <Fuente ruta="lib/agent/ask-user-question.ts" commit="8a690189" />

**Stay on track.** It can show a list of steps, propose a plan before changing the project and continue a longer task across rounds. The goal and what remains are part of its work. <Fuente ruta="lib/agent/plan-mode.ts" commit="8a690189" /> <Fuente ruta="lib/agent/goal.ts" commit="8a690189" />

**Retrieve context.** It can search earlier project conversations and return to decisions you have already made. Files and the project's state give it context to continue. <Fuente ruta="lib/agent/session-query.ts" commit="8a690189" />

**Check and correct.** It has a terminal to run code and tests, and a real browser to inspect and use what it builds. Execution errors and observed results give it information to correct a delivery. <Fuente ruta="lib/agent/verify.ts" commit="8a690189" />

Ask for a presentation about your project alongside a dashboard, for example: it can work on the pieces, inspect their results and keep them together for the next correction.

<h2 id="create">More ways to create</h2>

Len started by helping you build and change your website. With **Len 3.0**, that conversation can also end with a presentation for a meeting, a dashboard to understand your data, an image for your project, an animated video or a voice piece. You keep working in OpenLen, in the same project, without opening a different chat for every format.

You do not need to start with a website. Ask directly for a deliverable and open it in the center of your workspace.

<h2 id="saved">A piece you can open again</h2>

Each deliverable is saved separately, with its title, source files, versions and exports. Correcting a piece preserves its identity; returning to a project lets you find it again. Creating a presentation or dashboard does not replace your project's page.

- **Presentations:** a browsable preview and PowerPoint, PDF and HTML files.
- **Dashboards:** metrics and interactive charts, with identified source data and HTML, PNG and CSV exports.
- **Images:** pieces you can open and download in image formats.
- **Videos:** animated compositions with a preview and an MP4 file.
- **Voice:** text turned into downloadable audio. Videos and audio pieces can also include music and sound effects you upload yourself.

The preview fits a dashboard to the available space and slides to the window width, preserving their proportions. <Fuente ruta="components/workspace-v2/artifacts/artifact-viewer.tsx" commit="8a690189" />

<h2 id="terminal">A real terminal</h2>

In the previous release, Len's terminal interpreted commands within the project. Now it can **run programs, install packages and execute code in an isolated environment**. One account does not share its environment with another account's files.

This gives it more ways to work and check results: process files, run a test or prepare a resource the project needs. Environment usage is measured and charged as part of the turn. <Fuente ruta="lib/agent/sandbox/choose.ts" commit="8a690189" />

<h2 id="connections">Your accounts and your project</h2>

OpenLen also brings together configuration for **Shopify, Stripe and project email**. You can connect your store, configure your payment account and set up incoming and outgoing project email. Each connection requires your own account and the corresponding setup.

Credentials belong in their settings, without pasting them into the conversation or including them in public code. Connecting an account does not mean all its flows have been configured: Len should explain what it prepared and what remains.

Websites and apps retain their database, visitor accounts, files and real-time updates. The new deliverables join that workspace.

<h2 id="checks">How a delivery is checked</h2>

A queued job is not a finished delivery. Deliverables go through compilation and format-specific checks before becoming available. Dashboards bind their figures to the accompanying data; presentations and videos are rendered before the final export is offered. <Fuente ruta="lib/artifacts/jobs.ts" commit="8a690189" />

You can open the preview, download the result and request a correction to the same piece. If something fails, the deliverable's status should say so. Generated images and voice cost credits; generating them again is not a free correction.

<Abierto id="open" titulo="What remains open">
**Edge Functions are still awaiting activation.** This release therefore does not promise custom server code for every app or that connecting an integration completes all its flows.

Len does not generate music or sound effects: it can use files you upload. We have not yet published a comparison of intelligence or performance between Len 3.0 and 2.5; historical results retain their dates and context. Delivery checks provide evidence, not a guarantee that every project will work without review.
