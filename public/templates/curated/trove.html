FREQUENTLY ASKED

Questions makers ask before they leave Etsy.

How is the commission split between Trove and payment processing? +

Trove takes 8% on Maker (4% on Studio) of the item price plus shipping. Stripe takes 2.9% + 30¢ for the card. So a $100 sale with $12 shipping nets: $112 − 8.96 (Trove 8%) − 3.55 (Stripe) = $99.49 on Maker, or $103.97 on Studio. Every payout email shows the breakdown line-by-line.

Can I bring my existing Shopify or Etsy products over? +

Yes. Drop your Shopify export CSV or paste your Etsy shop URL — we'll import listings, photos, variants, and tags in under 14 minutes. We auto-rewrite descriptions to Trove's editorial tone in draft (you approve every one). Reviews don't carry over (Etsy doesn't expose them); we have a separate path for migrating those manually.

Do you offer printed packaging for makers? +

Optional. Trove Kraft is uncoated recycled kraft mailers + slip-cards starting at $0.42/unit (min 50). You can co-print your studio logo for a one-time $48 plate fee. Or use your own — Trove never requires Trove-branded packaging.

How are featured drops curated? Can I apply? +

Four editors (Hana, Marcus, Priya, Ines — all working makers themselves) curate 47 drops a year. Every Friday the next month's drop themes are posted; you apply with 3 pieces and a short note. Acceptance rate is about 34%. Featured drops drive ~14× a maker's average sell-through that week.

What about international shipping, VAT, and customs? +

Trove is registered for EU IOSS and UK VAT, so VAT is collected at checkout and remitted on your behalf. Customs forms are pre-filled from your listing's HS code (we suggest one when you create the listing). You print the label, slap it on, drop it at USPS / Royal Mail / Sendle / DHL. Studio gets discounted label rates — about 38% off USPS retail on average.
