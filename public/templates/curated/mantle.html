FREQUENTLY ASKED

Questions ops leaders ask on the procurement call.

Does inventory sync in real-time across channels, or is it polling on a cron? +

Both, by design. We subscribe to webhooks on channels that support them (Shopify, TikTok Shop, Faire) and use a 30-second polling fallback on Amazon, where webhooks are unreliable. End-to-end p50 is 640ms; p95 is 1.18s. We benchmark this publicly at mantle.dev/latency.

What carriers do you support out of the box, and is rate-shopping included? +

23 carriers via direct integrations plus the long tail through EasyPost. USPS, UPS, FedEx, DHL, Canada Post, Royal Mail, Yodel, Sendle, OnTrac. Rate-shopping is included on Scale — Studio gets static-rule routing only.

How does Mantle handle backorders and pre-orders without overselling? +

Per-SKU policy. You set a "virtual stock" cap that's the sum of on-hand + incoming PO with an expected-receive date. Mantle exposes that cap to channels, marks the line as "ships by Mar 14", and decrements it as orders come in. If the PO slips, we pause new pre-orders and email your inbox.

Does it forecast demand, or am I still doing that in spreadsheets? +

Mantle's forecast uses a 52-week velocity window per SKU, blended with category seasonality and your supplier's lead-time variance. We output a recommended reorder quantity and a confidence band. The agent will only auto-draft a PO; a human always approves the send.

Can I bring my own 3PL or am I locked into a specific warehouse network? +

3PL-agnostic. ShipBob and ShipHero are first-party connectors, but any 3PL with an EDI 940/944 feed or a REST API connects in under a week. We also support self-fulfilled warehouses with our barcode-scanning pick app on iOS / Android.
