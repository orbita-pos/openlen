How a day runs

10am to 5pm, no filler.

Live on Zoom · 24 seats
pre-reading sent 5 days ahead
10:00 Open & framing +
Twenty-four people, brief intros, and the operator framing the problem honestly — including where the easy advice on this topic is wrong. You'll also name the specific version of the problem you brought, so the day stays anchored to real work.
10:30 The framework +
The core teaching block — the mental model the operator actually used, with the real decisions and the real trade-offs, not a sanitized case study. This is the one stretch of the day that looks like a talk, and it's kept under an hour on purpose.
11:30 Working session I +
You apply the framework to your own situation in small breakout groups, with the operator rotating between rooms. You leave this block with a first draft of something real — an experiment brief, a pricing canvas, an interview rubric.
13:30 Working session II +
The harder half: the edge cases, the politics, and the constraints that make this difficult in a real org. Groups pressure-test each other's drafts, and the operator does a few teardowns live, in front of everyone, with permission.
15:00 Live teardowns +
Volunteers put their actual artifact on the screen and the room — operator included — works it over. Attendees consistently rate this the highest-value block; it's where the abstract advice becomes specific to your company.
16:15 Synthesis & an action plan +
You leave with a written, three-step plan for the next two weeks — not notes, a plan — plus the full resource pack. The day closes by 5pm sharp, because respecting your calendar is part of the product.

Good questions

Before you reserve.

Will the workshop be recorded? +

The teaching blocks are recorded and shared with attendees for personal review for 60 days. The breakout sessions and live teardowns are not — people speak candidly about real numbers and real hiring decisions, and that only works if it stays in the room. The recording is a backup, not a substitute: roughly all of the value is in being there live and in the work you do during the day.

Can my company expense it? +

Almost everyone does. At $499, a workshop sits well under most managers' discretionary approval limit, and we issue a proper itemized invoice in your company's name immediately on booking. We can also bill a company directly via PO if your finance team prefers that — just choose "invoice my company" at checkout, or email us and we'll set it up the same day.

Are workshops live every month? +

Yes — there's a workshop roughly every three to four weeks, and the topic rotates. Popular topics like pricing and hiring come back two or three times a year, so if the one you need has already passed, it will return. The calendar is published a full quarter ahead, and pack holders can see and book unannounced dates first.

What if I have to miss the live day? +

Plans change, so a seat can be moved to any future date for free up to 72 hours before the workshop — or handed to a colleague at any time, including the morning of. Inside 72 hours, we can roll your seat to the next run of the same topic. Because the format is one self-contained day, rescheduling costs you nothing but the wait.

Do you offer group discounts for teams? +

Yes. Four or more people from the same company attending the same workshop get 20% off every seat and a private breakout room, so your team works its own real problem together rather than being split across strangers. For a larger group or a topic delivered privately for one company, email team@roundtable.work and we'll quote a dedicated session.
