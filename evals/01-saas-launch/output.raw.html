What's inside

Everything Tide does in the background.

Six surfaces, one inbox. Tide handles the small things so you can spend the morning on the work that ships.

  • Triage in the background

    Tide reads every message and decides what waits, what answers itself, and what needs you.

  • Drafts that sound like you

    Replies match your tone from past threads. Edit one line, send.

  • Follow-ups, on their own

    Three days, no reply, gentle nudge. Two more nudges, then closed quietly.

  • Multi-account, one rope

    Personal, work, side project. One queue, no context switch.

  • Quiet by design

    No notifications for noise. Only the threads worth interrupting your work.
