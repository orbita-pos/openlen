Questions, answered

How accurate is the AI ranking?

FlowDeck uses project deadlines, blockers, and custom rules you set. Most teams adjust the model once during onboarding; after that, it matches their manual triage in over 90% of cases.

Is our work data secure?

All boards are encrypted in transit and at rest. We do not train models on your content.

Can I migrate from Trello, Asana, or Notion?

Yes. Import your boards via CSV or API; labels, assignees, and due dates carry over automatically.
