Questions, answered.

If the answer you need isn't here, write back. We answer in hours, not days.

Where do you source the coffee from?

Exclusively from small farms on the slopes of active and dormant volcanoes in Oaxaca and Chiapas, Mexico. The mineral-rich soil gives the beans their distinct brightness and depth.

How fresh is each shipment?

We roast and ship within 48 hours of order cutoff. Subscribers receive beans with at least 21 days of shelf life, typically arriving within 3–5 days domestically.

What do the $19 and $49 tiers include?

$19 monthly delivers 200g of single-origin coffee. $49 quarterly sends 600g every three months. Both include free shipping and direct impact reporting from the farmer who grew your batch.

How do you support Mexican farmers?

We pay 30% above fair trade rates and co-invest in soil regeneration with our partner cooperatives. Each bag includes a code to view your farmer’s profile and harvest notes.

Can I pause or cancel anytime?

Yes. No contracts or fees. Manage your subscription online or reply to any order email. Changes take effect before the next roast cycle.
