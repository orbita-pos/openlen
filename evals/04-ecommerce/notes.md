# 04-ecommerce — Volcánica (single-origin coffee) notes

**Score: 5/5** — Geography and pricing nailed.

**Brief:** "Landing page for 'Volcánica', a single-origin coffee subscription from Mexican volcanoes. Hero: bag of coffee on volcanic stone. Subscription tiers: $19 monthly, $49 quarterly. Mission: support Mexican farmers."

## What worked

- Hero: "Coffee grown in **volcanic earth**, delivered monthly. Single-origin beans from small farms on the slopes of **Mexico's active volcanoes**."  — brief honoured down to "active volcanoes".
- Real Mexican volcano names mentioned: Popocatépetl, Colima. Plus growing regions Oaxaca, Chiapas.
- Pricing matched exactly: $19 monthly, $49 quarterly.
- Mission language present: "guaranteeing fair prices and funding soil regeneration projects".
- Testimonials use LATAM-aware names + a believable specialty-coffee context.

## What didn't

- The "Save $8 yearly" line in the quarterly tier is mathematically wrong ($49 × 4 = $196 vs $19 × 12 = $228 → saves $32, not $8). The model invented a discount value that doesn't follow from the brief's pricing.
- Pre-fix run had geographically relocated the brand to Matagalpa (Nicaragua) and Oaxaca-only — completely off-brief. Post-fix is correct.
