Why volcano soil changes the cup.

High-altitude farms on active volcanic ridges produce denser, more complex beans. We pay above-market rates to the growers who tend them.

Volcanic terroir

Mineral-rich soil and sharp diurnal temperature shifts create beans with pronounced sweetness and structured acidity.

Direct farmer pricing
