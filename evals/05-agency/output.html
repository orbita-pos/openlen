Brewdog — Rebrand

Challenge: Refresh a cult beer brand losing visual cohesion across 50+ markets.

Approach: Built a rigid typographic system and reduced the palette to black, white, and Pantone 021.

Result: +32% brand recall in tracked markets within 6 months.

Patagonia — Europe Launch

Challenge: Introduce an established outdoor brand to urban European audiences.

Approach: Paired Patagonia's environmental ethos with local community storytelling and restrained print applications.

Result: 41% above launch-phase revenue targets in DACH region.
