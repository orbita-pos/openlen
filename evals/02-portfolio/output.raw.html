How I work

No endless revisions. No jargon-heavy decks. Just a tight process that gets to the right design faster.

Map the real problem

I start with your users, your constraints, and your data—never a blank canvas. Two days of structured discovery usually saves two weeks of guessing later.

Design the system, not just the screen

Every project gets a component rationale and interaction logic your engineers can build from. Handoff includes specs, states, and edge cases.

Refine with evidence

I test prototypes with actual users when possible, and run structured critiques when time is short. The goal is always the same: fewer assumptions, clearer decisions.
