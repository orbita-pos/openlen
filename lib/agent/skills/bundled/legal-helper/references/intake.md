# The questions of each kind of matter

One per turn, after their own account. Skip what they already told you. Explain why you ask anything sensitive.

**Housing (renting, a deposit, repairs, being asked to leave)**
- Renting privately, social housing, or something else? Is there a written lease?
- What happened, and what papers have they received (a notice, a letter, court papers)? The date on each.
- What they pay and whether they are up to date; repairs asked for, in writing, and the landlord's answer.

**Work (dismissal, unpaid wages, a contract, harassment)**
- Employee, freelancer, or not sure? Since when, and with a written contract?
- What happened and when; what they were told and how (in writing?).
- Any date by which they must claim or answer (these can be short).

**A contract or a purchase (a phone plan, a gym, a car, a service)**
- What was agreed, when, and where it is written.
- What went wrong; what they asked for and what the seller answered.
- Whether they want out, a refund, a repair, or to understand before signing.

**Debts and collectors**
- What the debt is, who is asking now (the lender or a collector), how they contact them.
- Whether anything has been filed in court or any money taken from their pay or account.
- Letters received and their dates.

**A fine or an authority (traffic, taxes, benefits, a permit)**
- Which authority, what the decision says, and its date.
- The date to pay or to appeal, as written in the letter.

**Family (separation, children, support, protection)**
- What is at issue, children and their ages, current arrangement, existing court orders, any hearing scheduled.
- Safety: any violence or threats. If yes, safety and emergency help come before anything else.

**Immigration**
- Their current status and any letter, appointment or hearing with its date.
- This always goes to an immigration lawyer or a recognized advice service: tell them so, and help them get their papers
  and questions in order for that meeting.

## Problems hidden in another kind
| They say | Also name |
|---|---|
| "The landlord said he'd call immigration" | housing + immigration + possible retaliation |
| "They're taking money from my pay" | debts + work |
| "My partner threatens me" | safety and family, even in a debt or housing matter |
| "I can't work since the accident" | possible benefits or injury claim |
