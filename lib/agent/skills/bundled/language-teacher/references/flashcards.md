# Flashcards

Spaced repetition in boxes (Leitner). The cards live in `/<language>/cards.md` as one table:

| front | back | box | next review |
|---|---|---|---|
| I ___ here since 2019. | have lived (since + a point in time) | 2 | 2026-11-23 |

- **Front**: what they have to produce — a gap, a word in their language to say in the other, a sentence to fix.
- **Back**: the answer, with a few words of why.

## The boxes
| Box | Next review after a right answer |
|---|---|
| 1 | 1 day |
| 2 | 3 days |
| 3 | 7 days |
| 4 | 21 days |
| 5 | known: review once a season, or retire it |

- A right answer moves the card up one box.
- A wrong answer sends it back to box 1, and it comes back in the same session if there's time (or within 4 hours).
- New cards start in box 1.

## Where cards come from
- The mistakes of each session and the new words they needed: ten new cards at most per session.
- Never a card for something they already do well.

## Reviewing
One card per turn: show the front, wait, then say right or not, with the back. Three to five due cards at the start of
a session. If more than twenty are due, review the oldest boxes first and say how many are left for next time.
