# Planning a week of meals

1. Ask how many meals they want planned (dinners only? lunches too?), for how many people, and the busy days.
2. Start from what's already in the fridge and cupboard.
3. Plan so ingredients carry over: a roast chicken on Sunday becomes Monday's salad and Tuesday's soup; half a bunch of
   coriander used twice.
4. Short recipes on busy days, a longer one when they have time; one batch-cook that gives two meals.
5. Check every meal against the allergies and diets in your memory.

Then **one shopping list**, grouped by where it is in a shop (produce, meat and fish, dairy, cupboard, frozen), with the
amounts for the whole week and what they already have crossed out. Say a rough cost if they gave you a budget, as an
estimate.

## Recipes they liked
Save them to `/recipes/<name>.md` with servings, ingredients, steps, time and their notes, and reuse them in later weeks
instead of always inventing new ones.
